The Real Hero's Journey We are born twice, the first time after birth, descending to this world from heaven like falling rain, and the second time when we dare to leave hell, Plato's cave, that world of shadows and illusions that appear to be the truth. We must cross the threshold of ancestral knowledge, such as that of Egypt, Greece, and Rome, reaching even the deepest confines of Space, to discover that our fire is like the Phoenix that is reborn, illuminates, and was always within us. In the end, we return not as the same people, but having been transformed. The adventure of your life awaits you now. Are you ready to begin the journey?
