From Search To Universal Service There was Buddha, meditating in the silence of the night, in the shade of the Bodhi tree, understanding the consciousness that cannot be described in words. The inner search initiated by the vision of old age and death is about to transform into a service to humanity. Thus, the worst that happens to us becomes the beacon that will light the way for others; learning, growing, and transforming oneself in order to then share the light with others. This is true enlightenment. Remember, we are not in this time and space to waste time, but to be of service to others.
